Dark mode email checklist

Email clients handle dark mode in three ways: some leave your email alone, some change only light backgrounds and dark text, and some invert nearly every color. You can't control which one a reader gets, so the goal is an email that still reads well when colors change.

The checklist

  1. Declare that you support dark mode. Add <meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark"> and <meta name="supported-color-schemes" content="light dark"> in the head.
  2. Write dark styles where they are supported. A @media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) block works in Apple Mail and some other clients. Elsewhere, clients apply their own changes.
  3. Make logos survive both backgrounds. A dark logo on a transparent PNG disappears on a dark background. Add a light outline or glow, or a light background shape behind it.
  4. Avoid pure black and pure white pairs. Off-white (#FAFAFA) and near-black (#111111) are less likely to be flipped harshly.
  5. Don't put text inside images. Text in images isn't adjusted, so it can end up dark on dark.
  6. Test it. Turn on dark mode on your phone and open a real test send in Apple Mail and the Gmail app.
<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">
<meta name="supported-color-schemes" content="light dark">
<style>
  @media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) {
    .body-bg { background-color: #111111 !important; }
    .text    { color: #EDEDED !important; }
  }
</style>

Frequently asked questions

Why does my email look different in dark mode?

Many email apps change light backgrounds and dark text when dark mode is on. If your email doesn't declare dark mode support, the app decides how to change it.

Does Outlook support prefers-color-scheme?

Support differs between Outlook versions, and several apply their own color changes. Design so the email reads well even when you don't control the colors.

How do I stop my logo disappearing in dark mode?

Give it a light outline or a light background shape, or use a version that works on both light and dark backgrounds.

Can I preview dark mode in Stampcheck?

Not yet. Stampcheck flags missing dark mode rules; test the final email on a phone with dark mode on.
