Gmail "[Message clipped]": why it happens and how to fix it

By Eray, who builds Stampcheck · Published 4 November 2026

Short answer: Gmail cuts off any email whose HTML is larger than about 102 KB. Everything after the cut, often your footer, unsubscribe link and open-tracking pixel, hides behind "View entire message". Shrink the HTML, not the content: remove repeated inline styles, comments and whitespace, and leave headroom for the tracking your email platform adds.

Why it matters

  • Readers rarely click "View entire message", so the end of your email goes unseen.
  • The unsubscribe link is usually at the bottom, which can push people to the spam button instead.
  • If the tracking pixel is below the cut, opens are undercounted.

How to get under 102 KB

  1. Measure the final HTML. The size that counts is the HTML Gmail receives, after your platform adds tracking links and personalization. Leave 10–15 KB of headroom.
  2. Remove comments and extra whitespace. Builders and templates often leave a lot of both. Keep Outlook conditional comments (<!--[if mso]>); they are code, not comments.
  3. Stop repeating long inline styles. The same 300-character style on every cell adds up fast. Move shared styles into a style block (Gmail supports one up to 16 KB) and keep inline only what Outlook needs.
  4. Don't embed images as base64. Host them and link to them; embedded images are both huge and blocked by many clients.
  5. Cut sections, not words. If an email is still too big, it is usually doing too much. Split it, or link to the rest.

Frequently asked questions

What is Gmail's exact clipping limit?

About 102 KB of HTML. Images don't count toward it because they are linked, not included.

Why does my email get clipped when the file is under 102 KB?

Your email platform adds tracking links and personalization when it sends, which makes the HTML bigger than the file you uploaded.

Does clipping affect deliverability?

Not directly, but a hidden unsubscribe link leads to more spam complaints, which do.

How do I check the size?
