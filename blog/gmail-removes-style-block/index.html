Why Gmail removes your CSS (and how to keep your styles)

By Eray, who builds Stampcheck · Published 4 November 2026

Short answer: Gmail supports a style block in the head, but drops it entirely if it is over 16 KB, ignores linked stylesheets and @import, and doesn't support ID or attribute selectors. Keep the style block small, use classes, and inline the styles that must survive everywhere.

A setup that survives Gmail

  1. Put layout-critical styles inline: widths, colors, padding on cells, font sizes.
  2. Use one small style block for things that can't be inline: media queries for mobile, dark mode, hover states.
  3. Use only class and element selectors in it.

Frequently asked questions

Does Gmail support the style tag at all?

Yes, in the head, as long as the block is under 16 KB and the CSS is valid.

Do media queries work in Gmail?

Yes, inside a style block in the head, which is why it is worth keeping that block small.

Why did all my styles disappear at once?

Most likely the style block went over 16 KB, so Gmail removed all of it, not just the extra part.

How do I find what Gmail will drop?

Paste the HTML into Stampcheck: it flags oversized style blocks, @import, linked stylesheets and unsupported selectors.
