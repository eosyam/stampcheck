How to set email preview text (preheader) in HTML

By Eray, who builds Stampcheck · Published 4 November 2026

Short answer: add a hidden <div> right after the opening <body> tag with the preview text you want, followed by spacer characters so the inbox doesn't pull in the next words of your email.

The code

<body>
  <div style="display:none;max-height:0;overflow:hidden;mso-hide:all;">
    Your preview text here, about 40–90 characters.
    &zwnj;&nbsp;&zwnj;&nbsp;&zwnj;&nbsp;&zwnj;&nbsp;&zwnj;&nbsp;&zwnj;&nbsp;&zwnj;&nbsp;&zwnj;&nbsp;
  </div>
  …the rest of your email…

Writing it

  • Treat it as the second half of the subject line, not a repeat of it.
  • Put the important words first: phones show fewer characters than desktop inboxes.
  • Avoid "View this email in your browser" as the first text in your email; without a preheader, that is what people see.

Frequently asked questions

How long should preview text be?

Around 40–90 characters. Phones show less, desktop inboxes more, so lead with what matters.

What happens if I don't set it?

The inbox shows the first text in your email, often "View in browser" or an image's alt text.

Why add ‌  after the text?

They fill the remaining preview space so the next words of the email don't appear after your preview text.

Can I check how it looks?

Yes. Stampcheck shows the inbox line with your subject and preview text, and you can edit both in the preview.
