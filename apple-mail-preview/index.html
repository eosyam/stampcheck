What goes wrong in Apple Mail

  • Wider than the phone: a fixed 600 px layout forces sideways scrolling or shrinks everything on a 390 px screen.
  • No mobile layout: without a media query, columns stay side by side and text gets tiny.
  • Blue links you didn't add: iOS turns dates, phone numbers and addresses into links, often in a color that clashes with your design.
  • Dark mode: without dark mode rules, colors can be changed for you and dark logos can disappear on dark backgrounds. See the dark mode checklist.

Frequently asked questions

Is Apple Mail easier to design for than Outlook?

Yes. It uses WebKit, so modern CSS mostly works. The common problems are phone layout, auto-linked text and dark mode.

How do I stop iOS from turning dates and phone numbers blue?

Wrap them in a link styled to match your text, or add format-detection meta tags. Stampcheck flags text iOS is likely to auto-link.

Does the preview show iPhone sizes?

Yes. You can switch between common iPhone widths and desktop.
