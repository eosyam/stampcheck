    What Stampcheck is

    A free tool for marketers and email developers. Paste the HTML of an email and see, in seconds, what will break in Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail and on phones before it reaches real inboxes.

    What it checks

    • Rendering: styles Gmail and Outlook drop, Outlook button and padding problems, missing font fallbacks, image formats, Gmail's clipping and style limits
    • Phones: width on small screens, missing mobile rules, tiny text, iOS auto-links
    • Links and images: dead, insecure, shortened or mismatched links, broken image addresses, missing alt text
    • Accessibility: language, contrast, layout tables, headings, link text
    • Deliverability and compliance: unsubscribe link, postal address, preview text, subject, placeholder text, spam triggers

    How it works

    Everything runs in your browser. Your HTML is never uploaded, so you can check unreleased campaigns safely. Previews approximate each client; always send yourself a test before a campaign goes out.

    Your email stays on your device

    Stampcheck checks your HTML inside your browser. The HTML you paste, type or open is never sent to us or to anyone else while it's being checked, and it is gone when you close the tab. The only exceptions are paid fixes and checkout, described below.

    What we keep in your browser

    If you edit the sender name or address in the preview, or resize the panels, those settings are remembered in your browser so you don't have to set them again. They never leave your device, and clearing this site's data removes them.

    Paid fixes

    Checking an email always happens in your browser. If you buy a plan and click Fix, the simple fixes also run in your browser, and the rest are made by Claude, an AI model from Anthropic: your HTML and the list of problems are sent to our server and to Anthropic only to make that fix. We don't store your HTML, and Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models. Polar (polar.sh) handles the payment as merchant of record and receives your payment and contact details under its own privacy policy. Your license key is checked with Polar and kept in your browser so you don't have to enter it again. When you start a checkout, the email you're checking is kept in your browser until you come back, then deleted.

    What we count

    With your consent, we use Google Analytics to count anonymous usage events, such as how many checks run and which buttons are used, so we know what to improve. These counts never include your HTML. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your IP address and browser details only if you accept; if you decline, no analytics cookies are set. We turn off advertising features and Google signals.

    Hosting

    Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and time of visit, to keep the site running and secure. We don't combine these logs with other data. Fonts and graphics are part of this page. The only other company contacted is Google, for analytics.

    Your rights

    The tool itself doesn't collect personal data, and analytics data is anonymous usage counts, so there is little for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question about server logs or your rights under GDPR or KVKK, contact us using the details on the Contact page.

    Changes

    If we start handling data differently, we will update this page before that change goes live.
