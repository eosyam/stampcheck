What Gmail changes, and what this preview catches

  • Clipping: emails over about 102 KB get cut off behind "View entire message", footer and unsubscribe link included. How to fix it.
  • Dropped styles: style blocks over 16 KB are removed entirely, and so are linked stylesheets and @import. Why Gmail removes your CSS.
  • Ignored selectors: ID selectors and attribute selectors don't work, and a background color on <body> is dropped.
  • Images: SVG images don't show and embedded (base64) images are blocked. What to use instead of SVG.
  • The inbox line: missing or short preview text, and subjects that get cut off on phones.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my email look different in Gmail than in my editor?

Gmail rewrites email HTML: it removes some CSS, ignores some selectors and clips long messages. Your editor shows the original.

What is the Gmail size limit?

Gmail clips messages whose HTML is larger than about 102 KB. Tracking links your email platform adds can push you over, so leave some headroom.

Does it cover the Gmail app?

The checks cover Gmail's rules on the web and in the apps; the preview lets you switch between phone and desktop sizes.

Is it free?

Yes, and the check runs in your browser.
